WaterMapCore v0.6.7 — Unified Topology Repair

- Added tools/topology-repair.js.
- Unified the old topology-check/snap-edit entry points into one toolbar button: Topology Repair.
- Scan is non-destructive.
- Meter -> Service: preserves meter location and can move a nearby service endpoint to the meter.
- Service -> Main: can snap the closest service endpoint exactly to the main-pipe segment.
- Main -> Main: repairs only near-miss endpoints; legitimate dead ends are not treated as errors.
- Safe small gaps are Auto Fix; ambiguous/large gaps are Review only.
- Apply performs one Undo snapshot, rerenders touched layers, marks the project dirty, then re-scans.
- Existing topology-validator and topology-snap-editor APIs remain in source for compatibility, but their separate toolbar buttons are removed.
