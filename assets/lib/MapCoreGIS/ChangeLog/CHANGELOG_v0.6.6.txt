WaterMapCore v0.6.6

Demand Allocation + Hydraulic Junction Generation

- Replaced the v0.6.5 one-branch/one-Junction concept.
- Builds noded branch topology with endpoint-to-segment T-junction support.
- Keeps shape vertices as geometry only; hydraulic corridors are split at roots/branches/dead-ends.
- Collapses short customer service spurs back onto their feeding distribution corridor.
- Projects meter demand onto the hydraulic corridor and clusters meters by network chainage.
- Default max 3 meters per Junction; balanced grouping means 4 meters become 2+2.
- Spatial cluster limits: gap and maximum cluster length.
- Junction position uses demand-weighted chainage along the pipe corridor.
- Demand balance is checked in Preview: SUM(input demand) == SUM(generated Junction demand).
- Existing generated layer can be rebuilt; original Junction layer is never overwritten.
