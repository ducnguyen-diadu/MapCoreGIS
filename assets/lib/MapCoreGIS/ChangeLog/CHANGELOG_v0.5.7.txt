WaterMapCore v0.5.7 - Manual Snap Editor

- Replaced Geoman vertex dragging inside Topology Snap Editor with a custom pointer-drag implementation.
- Vertex handles are rendered by the snap module itself.
- During drag, map panning is disabled.
- Snap distance is evaluated in current screen pixels via latLngToLayerPoint.
- The exact nearest point on the target segment is both the visual preview and the persisted WGS84 coordinate.
- No geometry is read back from Geoman after drag/save.
- GeoJSON remains authoritative and the source layer is re-rendered from persisted GeoJSON.
