WaterMapCore v0.6.2 — Spatial multi-layer + keyboard modes

Focused changes only:
- Space (hold) = temporary Pan. Releasing Space returns to the previous mouse mode.
- M = Select mode.
- V = Spatial Update mode (requires active edit session).
- Spatial Update rectangle selection scans ALL visible layers and may select many vertices/features at once.
- Selected vertices from different layers move together in one drag.
- Dragging a segment/vertex that is already part of a multi-selection keeps and moves the whole selection.
- Shift + rectangle adds spatial vertices; Ctrl + rectangle toggles them.
- No changes to Topology Checker, Create/Add New, Style, Recent or import/export logic.
